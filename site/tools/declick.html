01 / Understand

How it works.

Declick detects unusually sharp changes and interpolates very short flagged runs. Its 64-sample look-ahead repairs runs up to 32 samples; it is not for long dropouts.

Crackling source→Declick→Output

Use it in AudioRouter

  1. Loop a passage containing an isolated unwanted click and wanted sharp sounds.
  2. Start at Threshold 50%, then lower it gradually if the click remains.
  3. Raise Threshold if consonants or percussion become dull.

What success looks like

Exceptional short impulses are reduced while ordinary attacks remain recognizable.

If it does not work

Persistent gaps, clipping or driver dropouts require fixing the source; a click repair tool cannot reconstruct missing passages.

Select the node to open Properties. Click its name to rename it. While playing, supported parameter-only edits save after a short pause. For structural edits, use Save when the canvas marks a change. Enabled and Bypass are distinct live controls; see the in-app help for their behavior.

04 / Examples

Example setups.

Example 1

Remove isolated crackles

  1. Begin at Threshold 50%.
  2. Lower it gradually while replaying the same click.
  3. Check consonants and percussion for unwanted softening.
Example 2

Protect sharp wanted sound

  1. If drum hits or speech consonants soften, raise Threshold.
  2. Compare the same short passage with bypass.

These are starting points. Compare at a comfortable monitoring level and adjust for your own source.

05 / Troubleshooting

Common mistakes.

  • Lower threshold means more detection, not less.
  • Long crackles and missing blocks may exceed the repair window.
