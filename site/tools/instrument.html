01 / Understand

How it works.

An instrument makes sound from notes instead of changing incoming audio. It has an output and no input. Notes come from the on-screen keyboard, a connected MIDI keyboard, or a script or assistant.

Notes→Chosen VST instrument→Meter → Output

Use it in AudioRouter

  1. Scan your plugin folder and add the instrument from the Instruments list.
  2. Connect its output to a Meter and an output, then Save and Play.
  3. Press keys on the on-screen keyboard, or choose a MIDI device under Notes come from.
  4. Use Stop all notes if anything keeps sounding.

What success looks like

The meter moves while a key is held and falls back after you release it.

If it does not work

No sound with a moving keyboard usually means the route is stopped, the output is not chosen, or the instrument needs its vendor licence app signed in.

Select the node to open Properties. Click its name to rename it. While playing, supported parameter-only edits save after a short pause. For structural edits, use Save when the canvas marks a change. Enabled and Bypass are distinct live controls; see the in-app help for their behavior.

03 / Configure

Settings.

01

Scanned instrument

path

Scan a plugin folder, then pick the instrument under Instruments (VST2/VST3) and add it. The exact file you picked is remembered.

Range / choicesDepends on your selectionDefaultChoose explicitly

Try thisScan your VST3 folder and add a synth you already own.

Keep in mind Some instruments are only recognised after the first attempt to use them as an effect is refused; they then move to the Instruments list.

02

Notes come from

midiInput

Chooses the note source: the on-screen keyboard only, every connected MIDI device, or one named MIDI device. The on-screen keyboard and the API always work.

Range / choicesDepends on your selectionDefaultkeyboard

Try thisChoose your MIDI keyboard by name so a second controller does not also play this instrument.

Keep in mind A saved device that is unplugged stays selected and is shown as not connected; the instrument is silent until it returns or you choose another source.

03

On-screen keyboard

keyboard

Two octaves from C4. Press a key to play a note and release it to stop. The keys work while the route plays.

Range / choicesExplicit actionDefaultChoose explicitly

Try thisPress Play, then hold C4 to check the instrument reaches your output.

Keep in mind An instrument with a long release keeps sounding for a moment after you let go.

04 / Examples

Example setups.

Example 1

Play a synth to your headphones

  1. Scan your plugin folder and add the synth from the Instruments list.
  2. Connect Instrument → Output device, choose the output and Save.
  3. Press Play, then press keys on the on-screen keyboard.
Example 2

Use a MIDI keyboard

  1. Plug in the keyboard before opening Properties.
  2. Choose it under Notes come from and Save.
  3. Press Play and play; use Stop all notes if a note hangs.

These are starting points. Compare at a comfortable monitoring level and adjust for your own source.

05 / Troubleshooting

Common mistakes.

  • The keys are disabled until the route plays.
  • An instrument has no input: connect its output to a Mixer or an output, not after a microphone.
  • A licensed instrument can load and stay silent until its vendor app is signed in.
