Fun Voice Changer

Turn your voice into a character with presets you can tweak.

Visual walkthrough36 configuration controlsSynced audio walkthrough
See how it works ↓
∿

The tool always delays the voice by 21 ms, whichever preset is active, and outputs the same voice on both channels. A safety limiter keeps peaks below −1 dBFS. It is a fun effect, not voice anonymization: people may still recognize you.

01 / Understand

The idea, before the settings.

Pick a preset such as Pitch Correct, Deep, Robot or Radio, then adjust any of its settings. Pitch correction pulls your voice to the notes of a scale; pitch and formant shifting change how high and how big the voice sounds; ring modulation, bit crushing, saturation, whisper, tone, modulation and space add character.

Microphone chain→Fun Voice Changer→Cable to Discord

Use it in AudioRouter

  1. Add Fun Voice Changer after your microphone tools and before the output Discord uses as its microphone.
  2. Choose a preset, press Play and talk; the inspector shows the note it hears while pitch stages run.
  3. Adjust the preset's sliders, or open the sections below for every setting. Reset to preset restores the recipe.

What success looks like

Your voice comes out as the chosen character, at about the same loudness as your normal voice, 21 ms later.

If it does not work

If the effect sounds broken in Discord, turn Discord's noise suppression off for the cable input. If pitch correction warbles, lower the noise gate or add Denoise before the tool.

Select the node to open Properties. Click its name to rename it. While playing, supported parameter-only edits save after a short pause. For structural edits, use Save when the canvas marks a change. Enabled and Bypass are distinct live controls; see the in-app help for their behavior.

02 / Explore

Make the behavior visible.

A synthetic sung voice is regenerated with the chosen pitch and formant, then given the ring buzz. It teaches what each control changes, not the engine's exact sound.

The example repeats until you press Stop, and control changes are heard immediately. The picture shows the tool's shape rather than time; follow the timing in Follow the sound below. This is not a DSP measurement or control for your running AudioRouter session.

04 / Put it to work

Start with a useful example.

Example 1

Sound like a tuned vocal

  1. Choose Pitch Correct (hard).
  2. Set Key and Scale to the song, or leave Chromatic.
  3. Sing slowly; raise Retune speed for a more natural sound.
Example 2

Make a robot

  1. Choose Robot.
  2. Lower Ring frequency for a deeper buzz.
  3. Reduce Bits for a rougher, older machine.
Example 3

Use it in Discord

  1. Put Fun Voice Changer between your microphone chain and the cable Discord uses as its microphone.
  2. In Discord, turn noise suppression off or to Standard for that input.
  3. Ask a friend whether the level matches your normal voice; adjust Output.

These are starting points. Compare at a comfortable monitoring level and adjust for your own source.

05 / Get unstuck

Small details that matter.

  • Discord's noise suppression can remove whisper and robot buzz; turn it off for the cable input.
  • Monitoring your own changed voice while talking is distracting because it arrives late.
  • Bypass skips the 21 ms delay, so the timing jumps; compare with Mix at 0 % instead.
