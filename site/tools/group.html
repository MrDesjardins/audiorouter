01 / Understand

The idea, before the settings.

A group is a presentation rectangle behind nodes and wires, without audio ports.

Related nodes→Visual region→Same audio wiring

Use it in AudioRouter

  1. Add a Group from Tools and move/resize it behind related nodes.
  2. Click its name to rename it, choose a readable color and keep background opacity subtle.
  3. Lock the finished region to prevent accidental movement while working nearby.

What success looks like

A named region makes the route easier to read. Audio still follows the existing wires; the rectangle is not an audio processor.

If it does not work

If moving or resizing is unavailable, check Locked. Keep the caption away from controls and wires.

Groups organize the canvas and never process audio.

04 / Put it to work

Start with a useful example.

Example 1

Organize without rewiring

  1. Add a Group from Tools.
  2. Move it behind related nodes and resize the border.
  3. Name it, choose subtle color and lock its position.
Example 2

Prepare an overview

  1. Use one group for each understandable processing region.
  2. Keep captions outside important controls.
  3. Use fit or tidy layout and check the groups still communicate the route.

These are starting points. Compare at a comfortable monitoring level and adjust for your own source.

05 / Get unstuck

Small details that matter.

  • Groups do not mix audio and dragging them does not connect nodes.
  • Groups use local presentation storage; session export does not carry them.
