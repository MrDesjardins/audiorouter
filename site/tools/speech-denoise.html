01 / Understand

How it works.

Speech Denoise adapts locally to background noise around speech without a Learn step. It is a classical signal-processing tool, not cloud inference.

Conversational mic→Speech Denoise→Compressor → Output

Use it in AudioRouter

  1. Place Speech Denoise before stages that amplify noise.
  2. Start at Strength 70% and speak normally, including quiet syllables.
  3. Reduce Strength if breathing, consonants or desired ambience become unnatural.

What success looks like

Background is reduced around conversational speech without a manual Learn step.

If it does not work

For music or delicate material, compare Bypass carefully; a speech-oriented cleaner may suppress sound you want to keep.

Select the node to open Properties. Click its name to rename it. While playing, supported parameter-only edits save after a short pause. For structural edits, use Save when the canvas marks a change. Enabled and Bypass are distinct live controls; see the in-app help for their behavior.

04 / Examples

Example setups.

Example 1

Start with a conversational microphone

  1. Place Speech Denoise before a Compressor.
  2. Begin at Strength 70%.
  3. Listen to quiet syllables while adjusting strength down or up.
Example 2

Preserve delicate material

  1. For breathing, music or soft voice that sounds thinned, lower Strength.
  2. Use bypass comparison and choose the lowest useful setting.

These are starting points. Compare at a comfortable monitoring level and adjust for your own source.

05 / Troubleshooting

Common mistakes.

  • A speech-focused tool may alter music or other wanted non-speech material.
  • Stronger suppression is not automatically clearer speech.
