01 / Understand

The idea, before the settings.

A cable has a playback side chosen as a speaker and a recording side chosen as a microphone.

App plays to cable render→Virtual Render Source→AudioRouter route

Use it in AudioRouter

  1. Install/enable the intended cable using Virtual Cables and identify its render/capture names.
  2. In the sending app, choose the cable's render endpoint as playback.
  3. Choose that cable source in AudioRouter, connect processing/output, Save and Play.

What success looks like

Audio sent by the app to the cable becomes an AudioRouter input. Direction is from app playback into the route.

If it does not work

Confirm the cable identity and sending app's playback selection; similarly named physical endpoints are not the same cable.

Select the node to open Properties. Click its name to rename it. While playing, supported parameter-only edits save after a short pause. For structural edits, use Save when the canvas marks a change. Enabled and Bypass are distinct live controls; see the in-app help for their behavior.

04 / Put it to work

Start with a useful example.

Example 1

App to processing

  1. Choose an installed cable in Properties.
  2. Choose the shown speaker name in the source application.
  3. Connect this source to tools and an intended destination. Save and Play.
Example 2

Check the two sides

  1. Read the side name shown by Properties.
  2. Match playback to an app speaker and capture to an app microphone.
  3. Use meters to confirm audio before changing other settings.

These are starting points. Compare at a comfortable monitoring level and adjust for your own source.

05 / Get unstuck

Small details that matter.

  • The cable picker refers to installed driver capabilities; it is not an install action.
  • Do not connect source and destination through the same cable in a feedback loop.
