AudioRouter vs Voicemeeter

Coming from Voicemeeter?
Draw your audio instead.

Voicemeeter is a respected virtual mixer with years of features behind it. AudioRouter takes a different approach: a canvas where you draw any route, with voice cleanup, crash-safe plugins and per-app capture built in. It's free and open source. Here is an honest side-by-side comparison.

Where AudioRouter stands out

Six reasons to try it.

You don't need to give anything up to try it: AudioRouter works with your existing devices and virtual cables, and never changes your Windows default devices.

⌁

Draw it, don't decode it

Sources on the left, outputs on the right, tools in between. The lines are the routing, and they pulse with the level while audio plays. There are no strips or bus buttons to keep in your head.

◐

Clean voice built in

Noise gate, denoise, speech denoise, spectral gate, de-hum, de-click, a 16-point EQ, a compressor and a limiter, in any order you like. Chain as many as you need.

▣

Plugins that can't crash your stream

Load x64 VST3 effects; a gated x64 VST2 adapter is available for selected cases. Each plugin runs in its own process, so a crashing plugin can't take your route down.

◎

Capture just one app

Route only the game, only the browser or only Spotify, and leave the rest of the desktop out of your stream.

{ }

Script it, or ask an AI

A local HTTP API, a command line and an MCP server let scripts, a Stream Deck or an AI assistant build and control your routes, under permissions you set.

♥

Free, and open source

There are no paid features, licence reminders, accounts or telemetry. The code is GPL-3.0 and on GitHub for anyone to read, build or improve.

Being honest

When Voicemeeter is the better choice.

Choose Voicemeeter, or keep it, if you:

  • record into a DAW through ASIO, or need the lowest possible latency on an audio interface;
  • control your mix with a MIDI controller;
  • want virtual devices included in one install, without a separate virtual cable;
  • already use VBAN apps across several machines;
  • run Windows 10 or older, or a 32-bit or ARM PC.
Or use both

They work side by side.

AudioRouter routes to any Windows playback device, and Voicemeeter's virtual inputs are Windows playback devices. You can clean up your microphone in AudioRouter and send it into the Voicemeeter mix you already have, then move over one route at a time when you're ready.

AudioRouter never changes your Windows default devices or volumes, so trying it can't break your current setup.
