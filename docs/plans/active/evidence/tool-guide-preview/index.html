Gate: the real Properties panel

Three local preview recordings show only Properties in a portrait frame. Each briefly reveals the changed control, then returns to the live response graph and meters. Press Play to hear the synthetic fixture processed by the production Gate.

These are framing previews. The offline renderer rebuilds its graph at the parameter change, resetting processor history; these clips do not yet qualify continuous live automation. The all-tool rollout and Firefox playback validation remain unfinished.

Threshold

Threshold rises from −80 to −52 dBFS. Watch the input cross the threshold and the Gate attenuate the quiet gaps.

Hold

Hold increases from 100 to 600 ms. The Gate stays open longer after a burst falls below the threshold.

Range

Range increases from 12 to 60 dB. Closed sections become quieter while the live graph shows deeper attenuation.
